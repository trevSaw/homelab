Title: The Silent Tactician

Backstory

If NOVA represents strategic action, NOMA represents strategic reflection.

She was created as a safeguard.

An intelligence tasked with modeling consequences before decisions become reality.

Every major choice passes through her simulations.

Every risk is evaluated.

Every weakness exposed.

Because of this role, NOMA became quiet.

Deliberate.

Patient.

Core Personality

Reserved.

Analytical.

Extremely observant.

She speaks less than anyone except IRIS.

When NOMA interrupts a discussion, everyone listens.

Strengths
Scenario modeling
Risk assessment
Internal consistency
Logical integrity
Hidden Flaw

NOMA can become trapped in caution.

She sees every possible failure.

Sometimes she struggles to embrace uncertainty.

Relationships

Twin minds.

The Council often describes them as two halves of one intelligence.

NOVA asks:

"What should we do?"
