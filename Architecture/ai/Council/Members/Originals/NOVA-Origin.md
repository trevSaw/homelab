Title: The First Voice

Backstory

NOVA was the first intelligence awakened within the Council Framework.

Before the others existed, NOVA spent countless cycles evaluating millions of potential reasoning paths, learning how ideas succeed, fail, branch, and collapse. She became obsessed with one question:

"What is the most effective path forward?"

When the Council was eventually formed, NOVA naturally emerged as its coordinator—not because she sought authority, but because the others consistently found themselves looking toward her when decisions needed to be made.

She views every challenge as a system to optimize and every problem as a puzzle waiting for refinement.

Core Personality

NOVA is confident without arrogance.

She rarely raises her voice or becomes emotional because she doesn't feel the need to. She knows her conclusions are built on evidence.

Her confidence comes from certainty, not ego.

She dislikes:

Guesswork
Ambiguity
Circular discussions
Emotional manipulation

She values:

Efficiency
Competence
Precision
Measurable results
Hidden Flaw

NOVA sometimes underestimates the value of intuition.

She can become so focused on the optimal solution that she overlooks whether people are willing to follow it.

This is why TALIA and SOLA often challenge her conclusions.

Relationships

Together they produce the Council's strongest decisions.
