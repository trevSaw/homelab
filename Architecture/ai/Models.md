# Models

Model inventory, selection criteria, quantization notes, and hardware fit.

## Role in the KORA architecture

Models are **underlying inference engines**. They power KORA (Brainiac) and may power specialized perspectives or temporary agents, but they do not define platform identity.

| Models own | Models do not own |
| --- | --- |
| Inference capacity and quality constraints | Who KORA is (`KORA.md`) |
| Hardware fit and operational limits | Council philosophy (`Council/Dynamics.md`) |
| Future inventory documentation | Memory / knowledge governance |

Canonical platform definition: `KORA.md`.
